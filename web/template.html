Flag the instability while it is still intermittent

A lean premixed flame drifts from steady burning, through short bursts of sound, into a locked limit cycle. We train classifiers on 50 ms windows of chamber pressure and flame light to sound the alarm during the bursts, before the full oscillation forms.

How the data and numbers were produced

Method

1 · Data: a reduced-order combustor model

No public dataset matches a 15-run φ-sweep of a swirl combustor with synchronised p′ and q′. So we simulate one acoustic mode as a noise-driven oscillator coupled to an n-τ flame, the standard low-order description of turbulent thermoacoustic systems.

da/dt = (ν + η(t)/(1+|a|²/a²lock)) a + c₃|a|²a − c₅|a|⁴a + σ ξ(t)
ν(φ) = β cos θ(φ) − α,   θ = ω τ,   τ ∝ 1/U
p′ = Re{a eiΦ} + harmonic + turbulence + 2nd mode
q′ = Re{a ei(Φ−θ)} + flame noise

Leaning the mixture by adding air shortens the flame delay τ, so θ slides from about 105° to 30°, cos θ turns positive, and the Rayleigh driving overtakes damping. η(t) is the turbulent fluctuation of flame gain (std sν = 25 s⁻¹, 0.1 s correlation). It is what produces bursts before the mean growth rate turns positive. The subcritical terms make bursts grow quickly once started. The sensor sees p′ through a 0.5 ms waveguide, which the phase feature calibrates out.

Labels come from the physics, not from loudness. That is what makes early warning a real prediction problem: a calm window inside the transition is still labelled Transition.

3 · Models

Deep models are written in JAX/Flax and trained on CPU (AdamW, cosine schedule, 3000 steps). The CNN encoder is four strided 1-D convolutions with LayerNorm and GELU, followed by mean and max pooling.

4 · Metrics

  • Accuracy, macro-F1, recall: window level, against the physics label.
  • False-alarm rate: share of truly stable windows flagged as Transition or Unstable.
  • Operating point: each probabilistic model raises "non-stable" only above a threshold chosen on the 2 validation runs so that at most 5% of stable windows are flagged.
  • Alarm: on after 3 consecutive non-stable decisions (30 ms), off after 10 stable ones.
  • Full-instability onset: first window where p′rms ≥ 1.2 kPa and stays there for 90% of the next 300 ms.
  • Warning time: onset time minus the start of the alarm episode active at onset, counted from the true transition start at the earliest. Negative means the alarm came after onset.
  • Latency: one decision on one CPU core, features included.

5 · Limits of this study

  • The main dataset is simulated. The model reproduces the known stable → intermittency → limit-cycle route, but its parameters are tuned to the report's numbers (210 Hz, 0.3 / 1.2 kPa, φ 0.90 → 0.55), not fitted to a specific rig.
  • The real-data check uses 42 short recordings from a public student project whose provenance is not documented. They have no flame-light channel and no time history, so lead time cannot be measured on them.
  • The flame-image 2D-CNN track from the report is not included: there are no camera frames to train on.
  • The Transition boundary sits at a point where bursts are still rare, so even a perfect detector cannot reach 100% window accuracy. Warning time and false alarms matter more than accuracy here.

python evaluate.py            # thresholds on validation, test metrics, quiet-gap analysis
python real_check.py          # public recordings: in-domain CV + zero-shot transfer
